Record to Automate · journey screenbook

Sixteen static boards follow one person from first opening to coming back to a saved workflow. Every board keeps the approved dark studio, puts the screenshot at the centre of decisions, and gives one clear next step. When two states can't happen at once, each gets its own labelled frame.

This is the selected application design. The product decisions govern scope. The Mail example connects the boards; only the supplied Last Z screenshot is real, and later screens are labelled illustrations.

Real screenshot (one frame, CSS crop) Illustrative screen or step Example value or placeholder, not a default Proposal or open choice, not decided External system UI (illustrative)
Every step reads like a sentence

Each action says what it does in plain words, such as “Find Mail near the bottom right, then click inside the green area”. The coloured words match the marks on the screenshot.

Four answers, never a score

Found, More than one, Not found and Couldn't check always have their own icon and wording. Nothing is clicked unless exactly one target was found.

Editing, control and evidence look different

Editable marks have white handles. When the app controls your mouse, a red frame and a bar with Pause, Stop and the emergency key are always visible. A past run plays in a striped lavender “Replay” frame with dotted marks and a crosshair. It can't click anything.

Stage 11 · Failure: stop and explain

Stop, say what was expected and what was seen, offer safe next steps
