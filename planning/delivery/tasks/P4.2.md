- **Outcome**: physical-key steps record layout and keymap. A layout mismatch blocks replay. Text insertion is a separately authored step that uses a proven method (XTEST keysym mapping is a candidate), with IME and Compose shown as unsupported.
- **Depends on**: [P4.1](P4.1.md).
- **Files**: `native/x11/observe/observe.c` (layout capture), `packages/engine/src/actions/text.ts`, `packages/schema/src/workflow.ts`.
- **Steps**: capture the XKB layout at record time and compare it at preflight.
- **Contracts/errors**: `Blocked{layoutMismatch}`.
- **Acceptance**: `m4.keys.*`, including an AltGr case on a second layout under Xvfb.
- **Artifacts**: per-case ledgers.
- **Out of scope / escalate**: IME.
