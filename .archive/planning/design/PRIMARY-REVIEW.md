# Review of the selected Opus design

Status: selected by the user after mutual review and iteration. Opus v2 is the design direction for implementation. Astra’s alternative design artifacts have been removed at the user’s request. No unresolved High design issue remains in the sampled primary review.

## Opus v2

Read [design rationale](opus/design-v2.md), [review responses](opus/responses-v2.md), and revised mockup content across the journey. Primary rendered checks used `html_preview`: complete [overview](opus/overview-v2.html) at 728px; full boards 09, 13 and 15 at 728px; full board 11 at 1440px. Unlike some author previews, these checks used the original PNG, complete unpruned CSS, original SVG definitions and the document wrapper. No image substitution, narrow-width overrides, shortened captions or app text edits were applied. The overview measured 1,776px tall; all five previews captured their full reported heights with no console errors.

The sentence in the action inspector explains target, search area and safe area together. The revised editing handles communicate manipulation clearly. The replacement board now shows both the lost producer and the old read region against the changed screen; the separate saved frame names the unresolved step and blocks Run. The pause-for-help board clearly separates fresh observation from resuming desktop control. Consent preview excludes the desktop bar consistently with its label. The lavender ribbon, dotted evidence and actual-click crosshair make replay visibly different from editing and live control.

All seven received findings are accepted with concrete corrections. Adaptations preserve the underlying design goal without treating the reviewer's suggested control treatment as a user decision. The self-corrections concerning immediate Stop and search-only reset are appropriate. No unresolved High issue was found in the primary checks.

For user review: the sentence-based inspector and conspicuous control/evidence states make this the more visually explicit study. The stronger red takeover frame and lavender replay are additions for the user to accept or refine. The floating HUD covers some upper-centre game scenery; copy referring to an “empty” band is approximate and should become “upper area” in a production copy pass. Placement over the game versus outside it remains a design choice, not a proven capability. The long screenbook is useful as a reference; it should not become an onboarding tour that users must read end to end.
