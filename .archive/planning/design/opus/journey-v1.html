Record to Automate · journey screenbook

Fourteen static boards that follow one person from first opening to coming back to a saved workflow. Every board keeps the approved dark studio, puts the screenshot at the centre of decisions, and gives one clear next step.

Opus v1, independent design proposal, 2 November 2026. It doesn't replace the approved visual study or the recorded decisions. The Mail example connects the boards. Only the one real Last Z screenshot is real. Everything after it is labelled.

Real screenshot (one frame, CSS crop) Illustrative screen or step Example value, not a default Proposal, not decided External system UI (illustrative)
Every step reads like a sentence

Each action says what it does in plain words, such as “Find Mail near the bottom right, then click inside the green area”. The coloured words match the marks on the screenshot.

Four answers, never a score

Found, More than one, Not found and Couldn't check always have their own icon and wording. Nothing is clicked unless exactly one target was found.

Control and evidence look different

When the app controls your mouse, you see a red frame and a Stop button that's always visible. A past run plays in a striped lavender “Replay” frame that can't click anything.
